# 60–90 Second Live Pitch

Hi, I built this website concept for The Bookmark Cafe in Kalyan.

The main problem I wanted to solve is simple: when someone discovers a local cafe, they should be able to understand the experience, see what is available, find the location and contact the business without searching across multiple platforms.

The homepage immediately communicates the cafe's atmosphere and gives two clear actions: explore the menu and find the cafe.

I also added a filterable menu so visitors can quickly browse categories like pizza, pasta, coffee, momos and desserts.

The website is designed mobile-first because local customers are very likely to discover a cafe from their phone. The call and directions buttons are always easy to reach.

The final section focuses on location and contact information, so the website is not just attractive — it is designed to drive real visits and enquiries.

For the business, the value is better first impressions, easier access to the menu, stronger local discovery and a professional online presence that they can send to customers from Instagram, Google or WhatsApp.

If the owner approves the concept, I would replace the demo images with their real photos, connect the real Instagram/menu links, add analytics and deploy the site on a custom domain.
